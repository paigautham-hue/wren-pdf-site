# Wren PDF privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Wren PDF does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your documents never leave your computer.

## What stays on your computer

- Every document you open, and everything the app does with it (reading, searching, marking up, signing, editing, comparing, recognising text in scans, answering questions and writing summaries), is processed on your computer.
- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.
- Settings, recently opened files, reading positions, unsaved changes and earlier versions of your documents are stored on your computer only. They can be cleared in the app and are removed when the app and its data are uninstalled.

## When the app uses the internet

The app works fully without an internet connection. It connects only in the cases below, each of which you start yourself. None of these requests contains anything from your documents, your questions, or any identifier for you or your computer. As with any download, the server you download from can see your IP address.

- **Optional model downloads.** Two optional features use a model that runs on your computer: matching by meaning (about 23 MB) and written answers (about 2.6 GB). If you choose to download one, the model file is fetched from huggingface.co. After that the model runs on your computer with no connection.
- **Optional text-recognition languages.** English is included. If you choose to add another language, its data file is downloaded from raw.githubusercontent.com.
- **Updates.** The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store and makes no update requests of its own. Versions installed outside the Store can check github.com for a newer version; the check asks only for the list of releases, can be turned off in Preferences, and nothing is downloaded or installed unless you ask.

## Answers written by a model

If you download the optional writing model, the app can write short answers and summaries from the passages of your document. This text is produced by a generative AI model on your computer and can be wrong; the app shows the passages it came from. Nothing you ask and nothing the model writes is sent anywhere. If an answer is inappropriate you can report it: see [Report a problem](report.md). A report is only ever sent by you.

## Microsoft Store

If the app is installed from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect installation and usage information under its own privacy statement. Wren PDF has no access to that information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact shown on the app's Microsoft Store page, or raised on the [report page](report.md).
